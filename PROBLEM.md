# RozgarAI — Problem Statement

**Track:** #10 Tech Employment for Youth — Code for a Billion (Bharat Agentic-AI Hackathon 2026)

## The problem

India has the world's largest youth population and the world's fastest-growing
developer community — but the bridge between learning to code and getting a
first tech job is broken.

### Size of the problem (how many people are affected)

- **371 million Indians are aged 15–29** — about **27% of the country's
  population** (PLFS / Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation).
- **17 million developers in India are on GitHub**, growing **28% year over
  year** (GitHub Octoverse 2024). India added **5.2 million developers in a
  single year** (Octoverse 2025) — one in every three new developers joining
  GitHub worldwide — and is projected to become the **world's largest
  developer community by 2028**.
- NASSCOM expects **~2.7 million new and reskilled AI jobs by 2028**.

### Severity of the problem (how much it hurts)

- The youth unemployment rate (ages 15–29) was **10.2% in 2023-24**
  (PLFS, MoSPI) — more than **3x the overall unemployment rate of 3.2%**.
  In urban India it is **14.7%**; for educated youth (secondary education
  and above) it is **15.4%**.
- Youth labour-force participation is only **46.5%**, versus 60.1% for the
  working-age population — millions of young people are not even in the
  labour force.
- The India Skills Report 2026 found **44 out of every 100 graduates are
  not employable** — degrees do not translate into job-ready proof of skill.
- The gap is a **proof gap, not a talent gap**: a beginner coder's GitHub
  profile is scattered, unranked and invisible to recruiters; entry-level
  jobs are scattered across dozens of portals; and there is no guided plan
  that turns "what I know" into "what this job needs".

### The policy moment

- On 15 August 2026 (80th Independence Day address), Prime Minister
  Narendra Modi pledged **AI skills training for 1 crore (10 million) young
  Indians over the next year**. The government's YUVA AI for ALL programme
  under the IndiaAI Mission already had **85.27 lakh enrolments as of
  8 July 2026**.
- 10 million newly AI-trained youth will need exactly what RozgarAI does:
  a way to **prove** their skills, **find** matching first jobs, and **close**
  the remaining skill gap — at scale, in minutes, for free.

## What RozgarAI does

RozgarAI turns a beginner coder's public GitHub profile into a job-search
launchpad, in one flow:

1. **GitHub Analyzer Agent** — reads a user's public repos, languages and
   activity and scores the profile (0–100) with explainable strengths and
   gaps.
2. **Job Matcher Agent** — matches the profile against entry-level jobs and
   internships in Indian tech, with a match score and a reason for each
   match.
3. **Portfolio Builder Agent** — generates a one-page portfolio site from
   the GitHub data, ready to share with recruiters.
4. **Skill-Gap Plan Agent** — builds a 4-week learning plan from the gap
   between the profile and the matched jobs, using free learning resources.

## Why this wins

- **Measurable impact, fast:** the agent can run against real public GitHub
  profiles of beginner developers and report real numbers (profiles
  analysed, portfolios generated, jobs matched, average match score) —
  the judging rubric's tie-breaker is proven impact, and ours is data, not
  promises.
- **Zero cost to the user:** public GitHub data, free learning resources,
  free hosting. No paywall between a young coder and their first job.
- **Built for the policy moment:** it is the missing "last mile" for the
  1-crore AI-skilling push — training creates skills; RozgarAI turns skills
  into jobs.
